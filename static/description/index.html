Service Fleet & Maintenance Manager

Track company assets, log their services, and see at a glance which ones are due or overdue for maintenance.

Features

  • Asset register: laptops, vehicles and machinery, each with a responsible employee.
  • Vehicle details: the license plate field appears only when the asset type is Vehicle.
  • Service history: record each service with its date, type, description, cost, technician and state. Deleting an asset also deletes its service records.
  • Preventative maintenance: set a service interval in days. The last service date comes from the latest completed service, and the next service date is calculated for you.
  • Overdue flag: overdue assets are highlighted in the list and can be filtered in one click.
  • Mark Done: close a planned service with one button and the asset's dates update automatically.
  • Multiple views: form, list, kanban and search, with grouping by type and by employee.

Built-in checks

  • The service interval must be greater than 0.
  • A service cost cannot be negative.
  • Only completed services count toward the last and next service dates.

Asset types and service types

Asset type Notes
Laptop IT equipment assigned to an employee
Vehicle Adds a license plate field
Industrial Machinery Heavy equipment with scheduled servicing

Service types: Routine, Repair and Inspection.

Access rights

  • Fleet Managers: full access to all assets and service records.
  • Fleet Users: read-only access to the assets assigned to them.

How to use

  1. Create an asset under Fleet → Assets, then set its type, responsible employee and service interval.
  2. Add service records in the Service History tab of the asset.
  3. When a service is finished, click Mark Done. The last and next service dates update on their own.
  4. Use the Overdue filter in the asset list to see what needs attention.
